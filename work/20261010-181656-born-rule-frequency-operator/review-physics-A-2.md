## Issues
- [major] "the space of lists indexed by infinite strings (von Neumann's infinite tensor product" — false as written, and the next sentence contradicts it. Take any infinite string. For 0 < p < 1 its entry in Ψ_∞ would be lim c₀^(N−m) c₁^m = 0, because infinitely many factors have size below 1. So as "a list with one entry per infinite string", Ψ_∞ is the zero list. The overlap defined as "the sum, over strings, of conjugate entry times entry" would then give ⟨Ψ_∞|Ψ_∞⟩ = 0, not the product of per-photon overlaps (= 1) that the text claims. Von Neumann's infinite tensor product is not square-summable functions on infinite strings. It is built from infinite sequences of single-photon states, with the inner product defined as the limit of the finite-N products. Fix: "the space von Neumann (1939) built for infinitely many copies. It is not a list with one entry per infinite string (every such entry of Ψ_∞ would be zero). It is built from infinite products of single-photon states, and the overlap of two product states is defined as the limit of the product of their per-photon overlaps." Then drop "sum over strings" from the overlap definition for the infinite case. Keep it only for finite N.
- [minor] "at an ordinary 50/50 splitter (a half-silvered mirror) ... (Jennewein et al., 2000" — as far as I recall, Jennewein et al. sent attenuated LED light, polarized at 45°, onto a polarizing beam splitter, not a half-silvered mirror. Stefanov et al. did use a semi-transparent mirror. Please check against the paper. Fix: "at a 50/50 split: a half-silvered mirror (Stefanov et al.) or light polarized at 45° on a polarizing splitter (Jennewein et al.)".
- [minor] "a rotation of the polarization, like the tilting plate of an interferometer, mixes" — in the arm basis, a tilting plate only changes the phase of one arm. The sentence just before says phase changes keep every q-norm. The mixing in an interferometer is done by the beam splitters. Fix: "a rotation of the polarization, like a beam splitter, mixes".
- [minor] "Most many-worlds supporters have since moved to decision theory ... or envariance (Zurek 2003)" — "most" is an unsupported sociological claim. Zurek does not present envariance as a many-worlds derivation, and the list leaves out the self-locating-uncertainty route. Fix: "Many Everettians have since turned to decision theory (Deutsch 1999; Wallace 2012) or self-locating uncertainty (Vaidman; Sebens & Carroll 2018); Zurek's envariance (2003) is a related route outside many-worlds proper."
- [minor] Model 3 "Pros. The tail bound is three lines" — this is the only pro, and the cons are long. The standard pro is missing: unitary evolution alone, with no collapse postulate and no special measurement dynamics. Fix: add "no collapse postulate: the same unitary evolution throughout, so the frequency argument is not cut short by discarding branches."
- [minor] "A polarizer is a sheet that passes light whose electric field lies along its axis and absorbs the rest" — read literally, light polarized at 30° has no field "along the axis" and would be wholly absorbed. Fix: "passes the part of the light's electric field that lies along its axis and absorbs the part at right angles".
