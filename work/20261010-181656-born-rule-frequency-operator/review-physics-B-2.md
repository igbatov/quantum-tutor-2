## Issues
- [minor] "That scatter is the finite-$N$ face of the frequency-operator argument" — This overstates the argument. The text later says, correctly, that the frequency-operator argument on its own says nothing at finite N and needs the Born reading to get the scatter. It only shares the formula p(1−p)/N with the scatter. Fix: "That scatter has the same formula, p(1−p)/N, as the finite-N quantity at the heart of the frequency-operator argument, which asks..."
- [minor] "the record ... is carried by positions (of the scattered photons, ...)" — In the standard (DGZ) Bohmian mechanics the beables are particle positions, and photons get none. Photon trajectories exist only in some extensions, and the same paragraph's cons say the photon case is "less settled". Fix: "the record of which ions glowed ends up in the positions of particles in the detector and its records, so each run has one definite string."
- [minor] "with a share $p$ of ups" (tickets; the table says "a share $p$ of them 'up'") — This could be read as exactly pN ups in every run, which would give zero scatter rather than 5. Fix: "each ion's ticket is drawn independently, up with chance p".
- [minor] Figure b-projection-noise title: "the binomial spread the 1993 ion experiment matched" — It sits above an N = 1000 curve, but Itano et al. used a single Hg+ ion and Be+ groups of at most a few hundred ions. The caption hedges this; the title does not. Fix the title: "Quantum projection noise: the binomial spread (formula; Itano et al. 1993 matched it for the ion numbers they used)".
- [minor] "what 'chance' means when the formalism itself contains no chance" (Model 2 cons) — Under Model 2 the Born postulate is part of the formalism, so the formalism does contain chance. The intended point is about the unitary evolution. Fix: "...what 'chance' means, given that the state's evolution between measurements (the Schrödinger equation) contains none."
- [minor] Comparison table, Gleason row: "three or more directions" — The condition is on the dimension of the state space, not on directions. Fix: "a state space of three or more dimensions (three or more mutually exclusive outcomes)".
