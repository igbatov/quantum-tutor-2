## Learner correction (2026-10-07)
Learner flagged final-A: "quantum mechanics gives each of the two routes to a spot an amplitude" is false: each slit has width, so there are infinitely many ways through (and in the path-integral picture, every path contributes). Correct framing: amplitudes for all ways through one slit add into one combined amplitude per slit; the two-term sum is then exact. Adding the many ways within one slit is what produces the single-slit pattern.

## Learner correction 2 (2026-10-08)
Learner flagged final-A: "If you pictured tiny balls ... you probably expected a broad spread of hits with no stripes". The learner expected two bands, one behind each slit, which is the natural ball prediction. The text presumes what the reader expected and states the classical prediction without its conditions. Correct: balls travelling straight give two bands behind the slits; balls deflected at the slit edges spread each band, and with a distant screen and slits close together the two bands blur into one broad spread. In every case the ball picture predicts the sum of the two one-slit patterns: no narrow dark stripes, and opening a second slit never reduces hits at any spot.
