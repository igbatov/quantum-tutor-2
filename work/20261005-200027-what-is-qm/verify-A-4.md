## Notes
- Grouping by slit is exact because amplitudes add linearly: any partition of the ways gives the same total. The figures also assume that each slit's combined amplitude equals the amplitude with only that slit open. That is standard for the ideal (Kirchhoff/Fraunhofer) set-up. Exotic paths that loop through both slits break it only at a tiny level (Sorkin-parameter experiments, about 1e-3 or less). The text claims nothing beyond the ideal set-up here, so this is no FAIL.
