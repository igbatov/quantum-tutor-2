1. b-anim-one-photon (Experiment 1, 1995): the laser position sweeps, so the path separation d at the moment of scattering goes from 0 to 2λ. Panel A (schematic, not to scale): atom beam through three gratings, two paths separating then rejoining, laser beam crossing at the current position, an occasional photon leaving one path. Panel B (computed): arrival pattern behind the third grating for the current d, with mean one photon per atom, Poisson number per atom: visibility factor = exp(−n̄(1 − c(d))) with c(d) = sin(2πd/λ)/(2πd/λ) for isotropic emission (sign kept: negative c shifts stripes by half a spacing). Panel C: the visibility-vs-d curve with a moving marker. Show the dip around 0.5–0.75λ and partial revival.
2. b-anim-gas (Experiment 2, 2003): pressure rises step by step from 0 to ~3 p0. Panel A (schematic): C70 molecules flying through a three-grating Talbot–Lau interferometer, gas atoms (dots) whose number grows with pressure, occasional collision flashes. Panel B (computed): the stripe pattern read at the third grating (count vs grating shift), with average visibility exp(−p/p0) and total count also falling (molecules knocked out; state the assumed loss model, e.g. same exponential with a stated rate, labelled as illustrative). Panel C: visibility vs p on a log axis, moving marker, straight line.
3. b-anim-hot-molecule (Experiment 3, 2004): temperature rises from 1000 K to 3000 K. Panel A (schematic): a molecule on two paths ~1 µm apart, glowing more as T rises, emitting photons whose drawn colour/length indicates wavelength (red/long vs short), more of them at higher T. Panel B (computed): ideal black-body photon-number spectrum at the current T vs wavelength (log axes, as in figure b-hot-molecule) with the 2 µm line. Panel C: visibility vs T from a stated simple model: V(T) = exp(−K ∫ n_λ(T) (1 − |sinc(2πd/λ)|) dλ) with d = 1 µm and K chosen so V(1000 K) ≈ 1 and V(3000 K) is small (say ~0.1); label clearly "illustrative model, scale K set to match the qualitative result; not a fit to the 2004 data". Moving marker on the curve.
