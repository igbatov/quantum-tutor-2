## Notes for the learner
- A (double slit, waves and chances): suits you if you want the single most famous quantum experiment and a working picture of "amplitudes add and cancel". Weakest point: it is dense, and the step about why a which-slit record kills the stripes goes quickly.
- B (magnets, questions and answers): suits you if you like clean logical elimination and want the language behind qubits and quantum computing. Weakest point: you must accept "each atom is a tiny magnet" and some magnet physics before the first observation.
- C (atoms, colours and stability): suits you if you want to know why matter is stable and why the theory matters for chemistry and technology. Weakest point: Heisenberg's tables and the interpretations are too compressed to picture.
