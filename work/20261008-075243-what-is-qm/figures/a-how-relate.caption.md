How the models and views fit together (schematic map). Top: the wave function and the sum over paths are one theory written in two ways, and they always give the same predictions. Below it: Copenhagen-style, many-worlds and pilot-wave are three views that agree on every prediction for experiments like this one (pilot-wave given its usual assumption about starting positions); no experiment has told them apart, and which to prefer is still an open question. Copenhagen-style and many-worlds are readings of the standard theory; pilot-wave adds particle positions and a steering rule, and whether that makes it a reading or a separate theory with the same predictions is debated. Right, dashed: objective collapse is clearly not a reading but a slightly changed theory, with random localization added. Notice the difference in kind: its departures from standard quantum mechanics are far too small to see for one electron, grow with the amount of matter involved, and have been searched for without being found so far.
