How the models and views fit together (schematic map). Top: the wave function and the sum over paths are one theory written in two ways, and they always give the same predictions. Below it: Copenhagen-style, many-worlds and pilot-wave are three readings of that one theory; they agree on every prediction, no experiment has ever told them apart, and which to prefer is still an open question. Right, dashed: objective collapse is not a reading but a slightly changed theory, with random localization added. Notice the difference in kind: its departures from standard quantum mechanics are far too small to see for one electron, grow with the amount of matter involved, and have been searched for without being found so far.
