Computed far-screen pattern for the ideal set-up of the earlier figures (slits four times further apart than they are wide), with a recorder that leaves a trace of which slit each electron used. "Overlap" measures how alike the recorder's two end states are: 1 means it ends the same either way and records nothing, 0 means the two states can be told apart for certain. Bold: what is seen. Dashed: the two one-slit patterns added, the same in every panel. Top, no record: the usual stripes, with dark stripes at zero. Middle, overlap 0.5: the stripes are half as strong and the dark stripes no longer reach zero (the first one sits at about a third of the central peak). Bottom, perfect record: no stripes at all; the bold curve lies exactly on the dashed one, including the faint one-slit side bands beyond ±4 (under 5% of the peak). Idealizations: the recorder does nothing else to the electron, and the overlap is taken as a plain positive number (a complex overlap would also shift the stripes sideways).
