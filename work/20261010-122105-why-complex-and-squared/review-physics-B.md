## Issues
- [major] "which is the condition that the two energies of the coupled system come out real" — false. Real diagonal entries plus H12·H21 real and positive (e.g. H12 = 2, H21 = 1/2) already give real eigenvalues without H21 = conj(H12). Hermiticity is exactly the condition that sum |c_k|^2 is conserved, so motivating it this way hides that the "conservation forces 2" step assumes its own conclusion. The non-circular content is Banach–Lamperti: linear dynamics that moves chance between levels gradually can conserve sum |c|^p only for p = 2. Fix: "We also need H21 = conj(H12). Real energies alone don't force this (non-Hermitian matrices can have real eigenvalues). It is the condition that keeps sum |c|^2 fixed, so it is an assumption. What is not circular is the next step: for p ≠ 2 no linear evolution that moves chance gradually can keep sum |c|^p fixed (Banach–Lamperti)." Say the same in the "Why this account" and "How they relate" lines that call 2 "forced by the dynamics".
- [major] "is stationary (nothing observable about it changes in time), yet the light it emits" / "frequency exactly (E − E′)/h" — an excited state that emits light is not strictly stationary: coupled to the light field it has a finite lifetime. The line also has a natural width and a small recoil shift, so "exactly" is false. Fix: "In the Schrödinger model of an isolated atom a definite-energy state is stationary. Coupling to light gives excited states a finite lifetime, and the emitted line is centred at (E − E′)/h, apart from a tiny recoil shift, with a small natural width."
- [major] "No line sits at a *sum* of terms" (and the caption's "never sums") — false as written: R(1/9 + 1/9) = R(1/4 − 1/36) is H-delta at 410 nm, a real line. Also, a sum frequency (E1 + E2)/h, or "twice either level's frequency", depends on the arbitrary zero of energy. 1.19e15 Hz assumes the zero is at ionization; counting rest energy, it would be about 2.5e20 Hz. Only differences are unaffected by that choice. Fix: "Every line is a difference of two terms, and no observed frequency needs a sum to explain it. A one-real-number theory would make observed frequencies depend on where we put the zero of energy, which no experiment does." Rephrase the beats sentence ("nothing is seen at the sum or at twice either level's frequency") the same way.
- [major] "Classical particles carry no frequency and no wavelength: killed by (1)" — false. A classical charge in orbit radiates at its orbital frequency and its whole-number multiples, and that frequency drifts as it spirals in. That was the classical prediction for spectra. Fix: "A classical orbiting electron would radiate at its orbit frequency and its harmonics, continuously shifting as it spirals inward: not a fixed list of differences, and not a stable atom. (4) adds a wavelength that particles lack."
- [major] "it has no state that carries a frequency yet changes in nothing observable, since its square cos^2(ωt) pulses at 2ω" — false for classical waves in general. A classical oscillator x = cos ωt has constant energy, and circularly polarized light has constant |E|^2. Both work because the state is a pair of reals (value and rate, or two field components), which is exactly the article's point. Fix: limit the claim to "a single real number whose square is read directly as the observable", and add that classical waves with constant intensity get it by using two real components, the same move as the complex hand.
- [major] "differs from ... by ½√ρ ∂v/∂x, zero only where the velocity field is uniform" — the comparison leaves out normalization. A cloud distributed as |psi| has density |psi|/N(t), where N = ∫|psi| dx changes in time. With N included, the mismatch vanishes whenever ∂v/∂x is the same everywhere. A freely spreading Gaussian packet has exactly that (v proportional to x), and for it the |psi| cloud stays proportional to |psi|. Fix: "With normalization included, a cloud distributed as |psi| stays so only in special cases, such as a free Gaussian packet, and not in general; |psi|^2 stays so for every psi."
- [major] "Energies E± = ±ħω/2, so ω = (E+ − E−)/ħ" — sign error. The text defines + as the proton's moment along the field, which is the lower energy (E = −μ·B), so E+ − E− < 0 and the "Larmor frequency (E+ − E−)/h" is negative. Fix: "E+ = −ħω/2 (moment along the field, lower), E− = +ħω/2, Larmor frequency |E+ − E−|/h". Then redo the +x/+y algebra and the Check-yourself answer with that sign (the precession sense flips; the chances' forms are unchanged). Or define + as the higher-energy state and say so.
- [minor] "the position-dependent part is momentum times distance travelled" — true only when routes are summed at fixed energy. At fixed travel time the free action of a straight route is m·d^2/(2t) = ½·p·d. Its rate of change with endpoint position is still p, which is why the phase turns once per wavelength. Fix: "for a beam of fixed energy the hand turns by (momentum × distance)/ħ, once per de Broglie wavelength". Also: Brune et al. 1996 is a vacuum Rabi oscillation (the cavity field, not an external drive) with contrast well below 0 to 1. Say "oscillating" and name the idealization.
