## Issues
- [major] "shifts chance gradually from one level to another (that is what Rabi flopping and precession are)". The same claim appears in Model 3 ("none moves it gradually, as the ammonia inversion, the Rabi oscillation and the Larmor precession all do"). Larmor precession moves no chance between energy levels: the text itself shows |c_a|² = ½ stays constant. Chance moves gradually between the +x and −x outcomes. In Model 3, "level 1/level 2" of the ammonia matrix (H11 = H22 = 0, H12 = −A) are the nitrogen-up and nitrogen-down states, not energy levels. The energy levels are the stationary states 0 ± A, which (2) and the experiments section call "the ammonia molecule's two lowest levels". Calling both "levels" tells the learner that an isolated molecule's energy levels swap population, which contradicts the stationary-state argument the whole piece rests on. "The chance |c2|² … is the Rabi flopping of observation (3)" makes the same slip: the ammonia oscillation only has the same mathematical form as Brune's cavity experiment. Fix: in Model 3 say "two states (for ammonia: nitrogen above or below the plane; these are not the energy levels, which are the stationary combinations at ±A)". Replace "from one level to another" with "from one outcome to another (between atomic levels in Rabi flopping, between +x and −x in precession, between nitrogen-up and nitrogen-down in ammonia)". Change "is the Rabi flopping of (3)" to "has the same form as the Rabi flopping of (3)".
- [major] "Every frequency hydrogen emits is the difference of two numbers from one fixed list, the terms R/n²". This is an unnamed idealization. Fine structure and the Lamb shift split the R/n² terms, and the 21 cm hyperfine line lies inside n = 1, where the difference of R/n² terms is zero. The combination principle survives with a refined list of terms, but not with R/n². "Why this model" makes the same overclaim: the Schrödinger equation's definite-energy solutions "are the terms" only to this accuracy, since fine structure needs relativistic corrections and the Lamb shift needs QED. Fix: "To about one part in 10⁴ (ignoring fine and hyperfine structure), every frequency … is a difference of terms R/n²; with the finer terms included, every line is still a difference of two terms." In Model 1: "its definite-energy solutions give these terms (fine structure needs relativistic corrections)".
- [minor] "yet under it |c1|² + |c2|² grows". Starting in level 1, the solution is c1 = cos(2t/ħ), c2 = −2i sin(2t/ħ), so the total 1 + 3 sin²(2t/ħ) swings between 1 and 4. It is not conserved, but it does not simply grow. Fix: "does not stay fixed (started in level 1, it swings between 1 and 4)".
- [minor] "A short laser pulse puts caesium atoms into two nearby levels at once". This reads like the "in two places at once" trap. Fix: "into a superposition of two nearby levels (hands for both)".
- [minor] "each spin direction" as basis states: a basis has two spin states along one chosen axis, not one per direction. Separately, "turning clockwise at the angular rate E/ħ" holds only for E > 0; hydrogen's bound-state E is negative with the usual zero, and the sense of rotation depends on that convention. Fix: "each of the two spin states along a chosen axis"; "(clockwise for E > 0; the sense depends on the energy zero, only relative turning is observable)".
- [minor] Sources and facts. Griffiths & Schroeter §4.4.2 is titled "Electron in a Magnetic Field", not "Spin in a Magnetic Field". Kent (2010) is cited in the text but missing from the Sources list. "The ammonia molecule's two lowest levels" is Feynman's idealization: the 23.87 GHz maser line is the inversion doublet of the (J, K) = (3, 3) rotational state. Fix: correct the title, add Kent, *Many Worlds?* (OUP 2010), "One world versus many", and write "a pair of inversion levels, 2A/h ≈ 24 GHz apart".
- [minor] Figure b-model-2 caption: "The routes with |y| ≤ 1 (thick) lay their hands almost in a line". Over |y| ≤ 1 the hands turn by up to a quarter turn, and the thick curve is visibly an S. Fix: "point roughly the same way (within a quarter turn), so they add up".
