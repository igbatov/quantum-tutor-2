## Issues
- [major] "With rare exceptions ... there is then one photon in the apparatus" (set-up). This is false as written. A herald says only that a partner photon was emitted. Collection and detection efficiencies are low (in 1986 the partner left the cascade in an almost random direction), so most heralded windows contain no photon in the apparatus at all. The correct claim is "at most one", and that is all the anticorrelation argument needs, because α divides by the single rates. Fix: "With rare exceptions (two pairs in the same window) there is then at most one photon in the apparatus; often there is none, because the partner missed the optics or the detectors, which is why α is normalised by the single rates."
- [major] "half a photon lost" / "two photons from one" / "four photons from one" (meaning column of the S_p table). A p-total of 2 means the chances add to 2, so the rule is inconsistent. It does not mean one photon becomes two. "Half a photon lost" contradicts observation (a), which says whole photons are always detected. These glosses suggest a fractional or multiplying photon in an answer whose first observation says this never happens. Fix: "chances add to 0.5: half the photons unaccounted for", "chances add to 2: impossible", "chances add to 4: impossible".
- [minor] "sweeps smoothly from 100% down to 0% and back up" (item (b), under "What is seen"). As observed, the minimum is about 1% of the peak (98% visibility). The next sentence says so, but this sentence states the ideal as the observation. Fix: "sweeps smoothly from (ideally) 100% down to 0% and back up".
- [minor] "the interferometer with its plate, U(φ), is a continuous family of lossless devices" (model 3, opening). U(φ) is model 1's matrix, and it already assumes p = 2. Under a p-rule the device would have some other matrix. The observation is only that some lossless linear device moves chance gradually. Fix: "the interferometer with its plate (U(φ) in model 1) is, whatever its matrix, a continuous family of lossless devices…". Optionally add that by the 2×2 argument one 50/50 splitter, which is not a relabelling, already excludes p ≠ 2. The plate makes the point vivid but is not needed.
- [minor] "Critics of Gleason's route note that non-contextuality is an assumption ... (Bell made this point in 1966)" (model 3, Cons). Bell's 1966 critique was about hidden-variable (dispersion-free) value assignments. He argued that Gleason's additivity across incompatible measurement contexts is not a self-evident requirement there. He was not criticising Gleason as a derivation of the Born rule. Fix: "Bell (1966) pointed out that this assumption links measurements that cannot be done together, so it is not self-evident; he made the point about hidden variables, and later critics apply it to the Born-rule derivation."
- [minor] "In many-worlds and in objective collapse the hand is the thing itself" (remark after the interpretations table). This is true of many-worlds and of bare-wave-function GRW. It is not true of the primitive-ontology versions of collapse (GRW with flashes or a mass density), where the wave function governs something else. Fix: "In many-worlds and in wave-function-only versions of objective collapse…".
- [minor] "a real wave function (angle constant) would mean a particle at rest" (pilot-wave remark). A real wave function has angle 0 or π: it is constant on each region between nodes and jumps at the nodes. The conclusion (zero gradient wherever the particle can be) still holds. Fix: "a real wave function (angle 0 or π, so no gradient wherever ψ ≠ 0) would mean a particle at rest".
- [minor] Figures. (1) a-how-they-relate, left panel: the plotted exit amplitudes are the square-rule amplitudes cos(φ/2), sin(φ/2). Without a note, this is the same 2^{1−p/2}-style argument that the text calls circular on its own. Fix: add to the caption "amplitudes from model 1; an illustration of the theorem, not a proof of it". (2) a-interferometer-counts: the axis runs to 4π, but there is no hollow circle at 4π, where exit 1 is again 1. The caption also lists only 0, π, 2π, 3π. Fix: add the circle at 4π and to the caption, or end the axis at 3.5π.
