## Issues
- [minor] "These fixed amounts of energy, or \"quanta\", gave the theory its name." — Historically the word comes from Planck's (1900) and Einstein's (1905) energy quanta of light, and only later from Bohr's quantized atomic levels. The sentence makes it sound as if atomic energy levels alone gave the theory its name. Fix: "Energy coming in fixed amounts, or \"quanta\" (first seen in light, then in atoms), gave the theory its name."
- [minor] "What matters is that a record exists, not that a person reads it." — This is right, but it leaves out that the record has to tell the routes apart. A partial or blurry record only weakens the stripes; it doesn't remove them. As written, a learner could think any interaction at all wipes out the stripes. Fix: "What matters is that a record exists that tells the routes apart, not that a person reads it; a fuzzy record only fades the stripes."
- [minor] "why each electron ends up at one particular spot (the \"measurement problem\")" — A reader could take this to mean "why this spot rather than that one", which is a question about randomness. The measurement problem is really about why there is a single definite outcome at all. Fix: "why each run ends with one definite result at all, rather than a blend of possibilities".

## Sound points worth keeping
- The core rule is stated precisely and kept to a conceptual level: indistinguishable routes add amplitudes, distinguishable routes add probabilities. The dark-stripe argument ("a second way through made arriving less likely") is exactly the right evidence. "Observation" is correctly said to need no mind, and the article correctly says a recording interaction doesn't have to kick the electron hard, so disturbance isn't the explanation.
- The handling of interpretations is careful. Uncertainty is explained through the wave (Fourier) trade-off, not as clumsy instruments. Decoherence is correctly said not to solve the measurement problem. The figure specs check out: the sinc²·cos²(4πu) pattern gives 7 central stripes, the first zero is at u = 0.125, S(0.125) ≈ 0.95, and the curve 3 peaks are 2× curve 2. The C60 and mesoscopic-object claims are also right.
