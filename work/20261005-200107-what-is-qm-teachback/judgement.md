## Notes for the learner
- B (Atoms that play only certain notes): best if you want an everyday picture (guitar strings and the colours of neon signs) of why atoms have fixed energies. Weakest point: interference and the role of chance get only one short paragraph near the end.
- A (Dots that build stripes): best if you want the strangest part, odds that come from waves adding and cancelling, shown with one real experiment. Weakest point: the idea of an amplitude "cycling" with distance is abstract on a first read.
- C (Three-filter puzzle): best if you like predicting before you read and don't mind simple arithmetic. Weakest point: atoms and the two-slit stripes are crammed into one dense paragraph, and negative amplitudes appear there without any setup.
