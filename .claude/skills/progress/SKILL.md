---
name: progress
description: Summarize what the learner has covered, what they understand, what to watch for, which explanation styles they have preferred, and suggest the next topic. Use when the learner asks how they're doing, what to study next, or what the tutor knows about them.
---

Read `learner/profile.md` and `learner/history.md`. Then tell the learner, briefly and in plain prose:

1. What they've covered so far and what they seem to understand well.
2. Which misconceptions or gaps are still open, framed constructively.
3. Which explanation styles they have chosen when given a choice, if any pattern exists.
4. One or two suggested next topics that build on what they know and close an open gap, each with a one-line reason.

End by reminding them they can correct anything in `learner/profile.md` by editing it directly. Don't change the files yourself.
