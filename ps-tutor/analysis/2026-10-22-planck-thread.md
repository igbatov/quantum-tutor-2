## Patterns

1. **Every asserted step is checked.** A claim stated without derivation (2, 4) or an "equivalently" that joins two different things (1) draws a question. Analogies and restatements don't satisfy; a derivation or a counterexample does.
2. **Quantifiers and modal words are read literally.** "Would" vs "might" (11), "must have energy" vs "must have energy spread" (6→7), "contradiction" vs "prediction" (12). The learner uses the tutor's own earlier concepts to catch later overstatements.
3. **Understanding is tested by restating.** "So X?" (5, 6, 7, 9), including deliberately drawing an inference to see if it holds. After a correction, the restatement is refined, not abandoned.
4. **Domains of validity are checked** when theories or approximations are combined (8).
5. **Persistence.** If the reply restates rather than answers, the learner says so and asks again (3).
6. **Pointers are followed.** A theory named in passing becomes the next question (10).
7. **Consolidation is requested** once several corrections have been made (14).
8. **Form:** short, one point per message, often quoting the tutor's exact words in quotation marks, no pleasantries, occasional typos ("black whole", "fave function"), some articles dropped ("Signal with any frequency have").

Earlier in the same conversation (double slit, decoherence, Born rule) the same learner also: checked geometry against the figure (one-slit band centred behind the slit), asked why a far screen was chosen rather than a near one, asked whether mass splits between two waves, asked why probability is the square and not another power, asked for sources, figures and animations, and asked for derivations "without skipping intermediate steps".

## What did not trigger questions

Correct derivations shown step by step (the flux-conservation answer, the Born long-paper checks, the decoherence derivation) drew no follow-up objections, only requests for more of the same (pictures, details). Explicit status labels ("heuristic", "contested", "not verified") were accepted without challenge.
